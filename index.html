01 · Vision

From utility to cognitive partner.

Accelerated Knowledge Discovery (AKD) transitions AI from a basic utility into a cognitive partner in scientific workflows. It integrates specialized AI agents into the scientific process to help reason through different stages of research and accelerate science through Closed-Loop Scientific Workflows.

AI Agents for Science aims to build a thriving ecosystem for harmonized design, development, sharing, and reuse of AI agents for the science community — where scientists remain in command, and AI augments rigor instead of replacing it.

02 · Strategic Objectives

Four anchors guiding everything we build.

01 / Time

Reclaiming Scientist Time

Scientists often spend large portions of time on manual effort. AKD reduces friction in repetitive discovery, search, synthesis, and workflow tasks so scientists can focus on hypothesis generation, interpretation, and analysis.

02 / Orchestration

Cross-Disciplinary Orchestration

AKD connects information across data repositories, scientific literature, code repositories, workflows, and domain tools — instead of forcing scientists to work across siloed systems.

03 / Infrastructure

Uniform Infrastructure Integration

Common design patterns, SDK components, agent interfaces, guardrails, and deployment pathways support AI agents across NASA science systems and partner platforms.

04 / Trust

Scientific Integrity & Trust

Transparency, reproducibility, open science, human-in-the-loop oversight, and science-specific guardrails are first-class design constraints — not afterthoughts.

03 · Methodology

Design with CARE.

Collaborative Agent Reasoning Engineering — a disciplined, stage-gated methodology for systematically engineering AI agents for scientific and technical workflows. CARE moves away from ad hoc prompt tinkering toward reusable design artifacts, reviewed stage gates, and human-in-the-loop oversight at every phase.

CARE Repository 📄 CARE Paper (NTRS)
— Triadic Collaboration

CARE is grounded in a three-way collaboration where each role contributes distinct authority and review responsibility throughout the design process.

SMEs

Provide domain authority and scientific constraints. Final arbiter on correctness and relevance.

Developers

Ensure tool realism, implementation feasibility, and deployment readiness.

Helper LLMs

Support structured elicitation, artifact drafting, and phase-aligned review.

05 · Development Pathway

From design artifact to shared scientific tool.

Every agent travels a defined path. Teams first design through CARE, then build and benchmark in AKD Labs, pass an SME-led readiness review, and only then publish approved agents to AKD Flow and the public GitHub.

Phase 2 Build & Benchmark in AKD Labs

08 · Trust & Safety

Science guardrails — input and output.

AKD guardrails are not only input filters. They also inspect generated outputs before they are trusted or passed downstream — combining general-purpose AI safety, science-specific risk taxonomies, and defense-in-depth review patterns.

What guardrails detect

A non-exhaustive view of the failure modes guardrails actively check for in scientific agent outputs:

  • Hallucination
  • Attribution gaps
  • Unsupported claims
  • Outdated confidence
  • Overgeneralization
  • Lack of grounding
  • Multidisciplinary reasoning failures
  • Consistency issues
View guardrails on GitHub

Granite Guardian

Foundation-model-based safety checks integrated into the AKD guardrail layer.

Risk Agent

Reasoning component that classifies and explains risks in agent outputs.

FactReasoner

Targeted factuality reasoning over claims, attribution, and supporting evidence.

NASA Science-Specific Risk Taxonomy

Domain-aware risk categories tailored to scientific contexts and agent failure modes.

Defense-in-Depth

Layered checks across input, intermediate steps, and output — not a single gate.
